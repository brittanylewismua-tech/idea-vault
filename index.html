Add it to your home screen

  1. 1Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (the square with the arrow).
  2. 2Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. 3Turn Open as Web App off if you see it, then tap Add.
Keeping "Open as Web App" off means it opens in Safari, where you're already signed in to Claude. With it on, you'd have to sign in again inside the app.
